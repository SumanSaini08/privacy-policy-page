Privacy Policy for N8n-Automation

Last updated: 12 October 2026

N8n-Automation is a personal automation project operated by Muskan Saini. It receives WhatsApp messages sent to its WhatsApp Business number and replies automatically using an AI model.

Information we process

When you send a message to our WhatsApp number, we receive your phone number, your WhatsApp profile name, and the text of your message. We use this information only to generate and send a reply to you.

How we use information

Your message text is sent to a third-party AI service (Groq) to generate a response. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Storage and retention

Message data may be stored temporarily in our automation tool’s execution logs. We delete these logs regularly, and you can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your data, send an email to sainisuman8424@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and the phone number you used on WhatsApp. We will delete the related data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Third-party services

We use Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API), Groq, Render, and Supabase to operate this service. Their own privacy policies apply to data they process.

Contact

For any privacy questions, contact: sainisuman8424@gmail.com
